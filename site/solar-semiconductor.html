CAPABILITIES

Dedicated teams for every high-purity package

Each team is trained for cleanroom discipline and works to your specifications, weld procedures and test plans.

Orbital welding & UHP gas lines

Autogenous orbital GTAW on 316L EP tubing from ¼" to 2". Bulk and specialty gas lines, single-wall and coaxial, with weld logs and borescope records.

PP, PVDF & PFA plastic welding

Infrared (IR), butt-fusion and bead-and-crevice-free welding for UPW loops, chemical lines, double-contained piping, drains and exhaust.

Gas & chemical distribution hook-up

Gas cabinets, valve manifold boxes and panels, chemical distribution units and tool point-of-connection hook-up, with purge, vent and LOTO discipline.

Testing & commissioning

Helium leak testing, particle, moisture and oxygen (PMO) certification, hydrotests up to 110 bar, pneumatic and vacuum tests, DI water flushing and turnover packages.

Fabrication, skids & support structures

SS and MS frames, tool stands, pipe racks, gas and chemical skids, and wet bench sub-assemblies built to your drawings.

Skilled crews on your site

TIG, SMAW, orbital and IR welders, pipe and tube fitters, riggers and test technicians, with PROMAXX supervisors and safety officers.

SYSTEMS WE INSTALL

From the gas yard and chemical room to the tool

Gas & vacuum systems

  • Bulk gases: N2, O2, Ar, H2, He, CDA/XCDA
  • Specialty gases: SiH4, NH3, PH3, B2H6, NF3, N2O, BCl3, TMA
  • Coaxial (double-contained) lines for toxic and pyrophoric gases
  • Gas cabinets, VMBs, VMPs and tool hook-up
  • Vacuum forelines, exhaust and abatement hook-up

Wet process & water systems

  • Ultrapure water (UPW) loops and take-offs in PVDF and PP
  • Chemical lines for HF, H2O2, HCl, KOH and solvents in PFA/PVDF
  • Double-contained chemical and HF drain piping
  • Acid exhaust ducting and waste lines in PP
  • Wet bench installation and utility hook-up
